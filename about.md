# Meme Chaos

Random visual pranks while you play, plus a deadpan caption every time you die.

## What it does

- **Death captions.** A short line pops up on each death. It changes with your progress: early death, almost there, a long streak, and a win.
- **Chaos events.** Every so often something odd happens on screen. There are over 60 of them: word pop-ups, news ribbons, fake notifications, fake dialogs, full-screen scenes (crash, 404, no signal, game over, lock screen and more), a bouncing logo, a recording badge, black bars with captions, mirrored or flipped view.
- **FPS drop.** One event really drops your frame rate to 5 for about 3 seconds, then puts it back to what you had. Turn it off in settings if you want a fair run.
- **Sounds.** Uses the game's own sound effects with a changed pitch. No extra files.

## Settings

- Death captions on or off
- Sounds on or off
- Chaos events on or off
- Seconds between events (5 to 60)
- FPS drop event on or off
- Mirror and flip events on or off

## Good to know

- Everything is visual except the FPS drop, which makes the level harder for a moment.
- The game font has no Cyrillic, so some captions are written in transliteration.
- No faces, no copied characters. All screens are drawn in code.
